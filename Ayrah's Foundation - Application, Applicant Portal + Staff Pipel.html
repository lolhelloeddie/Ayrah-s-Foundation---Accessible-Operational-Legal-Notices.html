Apply for Help

Ayrah's Foundation helps people experiencing homelessness stay together with their pets. Tell us what's going on and what you need. This takes about 5 to 10 minutes. You can skip anything you're not comfortable answering, except the questions marked with a star. When you finish, you'll get a case number and an access code so you can check your status and upload documents we ask for.

    Privacy Notice for Applicants

    Effective October 10, 2026. Our full Privacy Policy is on our main website.

    • What we collect. What you enter in the application, plus any documents you upload when we ask for them.
    • Case number and access code. You get them when you apply. We keep only a scrambled version of your access code, so we can't read it back. Anyone who has both can see your case, so keep them private. If you lose your code, contact us and we'll issue a new one after confirming who you are.
    • Documents. Stored in a private, secure area. Only approved staff can open them.
    • Why. To understand your needs, contact you, and arrange food, supplies, shelter, veterinary care, and housing help.
    • Who sees it. Approved Ayrah's Foundation staff who sign in to our secure system. We do not sell your information.
    • Who we share it with. Only what's needed, with partners that help arrange the help you asked for (such as shelters, housing providers, or veterinarians) and with service providers that host our systems. We may also share information if the law requires it or to protect someone's safety.
    • Please don't include Social Security numbers, ID numbers, immigration status, bank details, or medical records in the application form. We'll only ask for a document when we need it to arrange help.
    • How long we keep it. Only as long as needed to help you and meet our legal and record-keeping duties.
    • Your choices. Email us to review, correct, or ask us to delete your information. We'll honor reasonable requests, except where we must keep records by law.
