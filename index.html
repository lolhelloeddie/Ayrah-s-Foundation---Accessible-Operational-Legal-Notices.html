Apply for Help

Ayrah's Foundation helps people experiencing homelessness stay together with their pets. Tell us what's going on and what you need. This takes about 5 to 10 minutes. You can skip anything you're not comfortable answering, except the questions marked with a star.

    Privacy Notice for Applicants

    Effective October 10, 2026. Our full Privacy Policy is on our main website.

    • What we collect. What you enter in the application: your contact details, general location, situation, pet information, and the help you need. If you create an account, we also store your email address and a password, which is protected by our secure sign-in provider.
    • Documents. Upload only documents our team asks for. They're stored privately and can be seen only by you and approved staff.
    • Why. To understand your needs, contact you, and arrange food, supplies, shelter, veterinary care, and housing help.
    • Who sees it. Approved Ayrah's Foundation staff who sign in to our secure system. We do not sell your information.
    • Who we share it with. Only what's needed, with partners that help arrange the help you asked for (such as shelters, housing providers, or veterinarians) and with service providers that host our systems. We may also share information if the law requires it or to protect someone's safety.
    • Your account. Anyone with your password can see your case. Don't share it, and sign out on shared computers.
    • Please don't type Social Security numbers, ID numbers, immigration status, bank details, or medical records into the application form.
    • How long we keep it. Only as long as needed to help you and meet our legal and record-keeping duties.
    • Your choices. Email us to review, correct, or ask us to delete your information or account. We'll honor reasonable requests, except where we must keep records by law.
